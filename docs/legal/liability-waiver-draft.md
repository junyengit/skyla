**Important: Please read carefully. This agreement affects legal rights and includes a release of claims arising from ordinary negligence.**

## 1. Parties and visit

This agreement applies to my ticketed visit to the elevated outdoor observation deck, indoor lounge, and visitor areas operated as “Sky LA” at 6100 Wilshire Blvd, Top Floor, Los Angeles, California 90048 (the “Visit”).

“Operator” means **[FULL LEGAL NAME OF OPERATING ENTITY], doing business as Sky LA**.

“Released Parties” means Operator; **[FULL LEGAL NAME OF BUILDING OWNER]**; **[FULL LEGAL NAME OF BUILDING MANAGER]**; and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents acting within the scope of their duties. No party is included unless accurately identified here or by a clearly defined relationship to a named party.

This agreement covers only the person signing it and any minor specifically listed below. One purchaser cannot accept this agreement for another adult.

## 2. Voluntary visit and authority

I voluntarily choose to participate in the Visit. I represent that I am at least 18 years old.

If I sign for a minor, I represent that I am the minor's parent or court-appointed legal guardian and have authority to enter into this agreement for that minor. I understand that all children must remain under responsible adult supervision.

## 3. Acknowledgment of risks

I understand that visiting an elevated indoor/outdoor attraction involves risks, including:

- travel by elevators and stairs and evacuation from an upper floor;
- slips, trips, falls, steps, thresholds, doors, glass, railings, wet or uneven surfaces, and reduced visibility or glare;
- wind, rain, heat, cold, sun exposure, changing weather, and windblown or falling objects in outdoor areas;
- crowd movement, collisions, or the conduct of other visitors;
- food or beverage allergens, hot beverages, caffeine, broken containers, and spills;
- illness, medical events, and delayed emergency access associated with an upper-floor location; and
- other risks reasonably associated with admission to and movement through an elevated observation deck and lounge.

I understand that this list is illustrative rather than exhaustive. I agree to follow posted rules and reasonable staff instructions, supervise minors in my care, remain within designated visitor areas, and not climb, sit, lean, or place objects on railings or barriers.

## 4. Assumption of inherent risks

I knowingly and voluntarily assume the inherent and reasonably foreseeable risks of the Visit, whether known or unknown, except to the extent a risk results from conduct that cannot lawfully be released under Section 6.

## 5. Release of ordinary-negligence claims

**To the fullest extent permitted by California law, I release and agree not to sue the Released Parties for claims for bodily injury, death, or damage to personal property arising from the Released Parties' ordinary negligence in connection with the Visit.**

This release includes, to the extent California law permits, ordinary-negligence claims concerning the operation or maintenance of visitor areas, premises conditions, ingress or egress through areas controlled by a Released Party, crowd management, staff instructions, and ordinary emergency response.

If I sign for a listed minor, I make this release on the minor's behalf to the fullest extent permitted by law. I also release my own claims arising from injury to that minor, including derivative claims and expenses, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 6. Claims not released

This agreement does **not** release or limit liability for gross negligence, recklessness, intentional or willful misconduct, fraud, violation of law, or any right or remedy that cannot legally be waived. It does not waive rights under the California Consumer Legal Remedies Act or require me to indemnify a Released Party for that party's own negligence.

## 7. Emergency assistance

If I or a listed minor requires emergency assistance, I authorize Operator to contact emergency responders and provide reasonably available identifying or medical information to them. If I sign for a minor, I authorize reasonably necessary emergency evaluation and treatment when I cannot be reached promptly.

This authorization does not require Operator to provide medical care and does not release negligent medical treatment. I am responsible for resulting medical expenses to the extent permitted by law.

## 8. Responsibility for my conduct

I am responsible for injury or property damage caused by my own negligent, reckless, intentional, or unlawful conduct, or by my failure to supervise a listed minor. To the extent permitted by law, I agree to reimburse a Released Party for a third-party claim only to the extent that claim was caused by that conduct. This paragraph does not shift responsibility for a Released Party's own negligence.

## 9. California law and severability

California law governs this agreement. Any unenforceable provision will be narrowed or severed only to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will continue in effect. Nothing in this agreement expands a release beyond what California law allows.
