# Sky LA launch-readiness checklist

Status: attorney-review draft  
Prepared: August 6, 2026

This is a business and drafting aid, not legal advice. California venue and consumer counsel should approve the final documents and checkout flow before payment is enabled.

## Do not enable checkout until these are complete

1. Confirm the full legal name and entity type of the operator doing business as Sky LA, its mailing address, merchant-of-record role, authority to sell admission at 6100 Wilshire, insurance, occupancy/fire approval, and rooftop operating authorization.
2. Publish final, versioned Terms of Use and Ticket Purchase Terms.
3. Publish a definite refund, reschedule, weather, closure, late-arrival, and no-show policy.
4. Add an unchecked purchase-terms checkbox immediately above payment. Store the exact accepted wording, policy version/hash, order reference, purchaser identity, timestamp, and rendered document snapshot.
5. Add a separate liability-waiver signature. Each adult guest must sign individually. A parent or legal guardian must sign for every minor; the purchaser cannot release an unrelated adult's rights.
6. Correct the Privacy Policy to match actual collection and tracking. Either disable Meta/Google advertising tags pending review or disclose them accurately and implement all required controls.
7. Define retention periods for abandoned checkout drafts, completed bookings, payment records, waiver evidence, support messages, analytics, and advertising data.
8. Verify physical accessibility facts before promising them: step-free entrance, elevator route, accessible viewing area, restroom, seating, service-animal procedures, drop-off, and evacuation assistance.
9. Keep the displayed price all-in. Current code correctly adds no mandatory booking fee; remove the obsolete booking-fee statement from the existing Terms.

## Complete before the first guest visit

- Publish venue and safety rules covering arrival windows, adult supervision, elevated/outdoor conditions, weather, temporary closures, prohibited behavior and items, emergencies, and lost property.
- Define the minor policy. The purchaser must be 18 or older; guests aged 13–17 remain minors even if they use the adult-priced ticket.
- Publish an accessibility statement and a working accommodation contact path.
- Publish photography and security-camera notices. Use separate, optional consent for promotional use of an identifiable guest, especially a minor.
- Confirm tax treatment, transfer/resale rules, duplicate-QR handling, support response ownership, and chargeback procedures.
- Remove alcohol language unless licensed alcohol service is a verified launch offering.

## Recommended checkout consent

Place the following immediately above the payment button after the total:

> **Before you pay**  
> Your ticket is for the selected date and arrival time. Visibility may vary with weather. If Sky LA closes your visit or the observation deck is unavailable for your entry, the Refund & Visit Policy applies. We use your email to create and deliver your booking; see the Privacy Policy.

Mandatory unchecked purchase checkbox:

> I am 18 or older and agree to the Terms of Use and Ticket Purchase Terms and the Refund & Visit Policy.

Separate waiver control:

> I have read and voluntarily sign the Sky LA Acknowledgment of Risk and Release of Liability for myself. I understand that every other adult guest, and a parent or legal guardian for every minor guest, must complete a separate waiver before entry.

Privacy should be acknowledged through a clear link, not bundled as contractual consent. Promotional email, text, and photo consent must be optional and unchecked.

## Draft refund, reschedule, weather, and closure policy

The 24-hour guest-change window is a recommended customer-friendly default and needs owner approval.

### Timed tickets

Tickets are valid for the date and arrival time shown in the confirmation. Please arrive within 30 minutes of the selected time.

### Guest-requested changes

Requests received at least 24 hours before the scheduled arrival may receive either one free reschedule, subject to availability, or a refund to the original payment method. Requests received less than 24 hours before arrival and no-shows are nonrefundable except where required by law.

### Sky LA cancellation

If Sky LA cancels a visit, Sky LA will automatically issue a full refund of the ticket price and all mandatory charges to the original payment method, initiated promptly and no later than 30 calendar days after cancellation.

### Rescheduled visits

If Sky LA postpones or reschedules a visit, the purchaser may accept the new time or request a full refund. Sky LA will process the refund promptly and no later than 30 calendar days after the request.

### Weather and visibility

Skyline visibility is not guaranteed and may change because of clouds, haze, rain, smoke, or other conditions. Weather alone does not create a refund right while the observation deck remains safely open. If Sky LA closes the outdoor observation deck for the purchaser's entire entry window, the purchaser may choose a free reschedule or full refund.

### Safety closures

Sky LA may delay, restrict, or close access when staff reasonably determine that weather, maintenance, capacity, building conditions, or an emergency makes operation unsafe. A complete closure is handled as a Sky LA cancellation.

### Requesting help

Email reservations@skydeckla.com with the order reference and purchaser email. Approved refunds return to the original payment method; bank processing time may vary.

## Privacy-policy replacement outline

The final policy must identify the legal operator and accurately disclose:

- Information provided by the purchaser, including email, visit date and time, guest counts, order details, waiver records, and support messages.
- Information Stripe collects and the limited transaction data Sky LA receives. Sky LA should state that it does not receive or store full card numbers or security codes if that remains factually true.
- Automatic collection, including IP address, device/browser data, referrals, page interactions, cookies, advertising identifiers, security logs, Meta Pixel, and Google tags that actually remain enabled.
- Purposes: booking fulfillment, payment/refund reconciliation, ticket delivery, check-in, support, fraud prevention, security, operations, legal compliance, analytics, and advertising where permitted.
- Service providers and other disclosure recipients by category and, where useful, by name.
- Whether personal information is sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising and the implemented opt-out/GPC behavior.
- Concrete retention periods or criteria.
- Access, correction, deletion, and marketing-choice paths.
- The adult-purchaser model and treatment of children's data.
- Security practices, policy-change notices, effective date, physical address, and contact email.

## Draft photography notice

Sky LA may photograph or record portions of the venue for security, operations, or event documentation. Sky LA will not use a singled-out, identifiable guest's image or likeness in advertising without appropriate consent. Promotional use of an identifiable minor requires prior consent from the minor's parent or legal guardian. Guests who prefer not to appear in event photography may notify staff so reasonable steps can be taken.

This notice does not replace a separate signed commercial photo release when Sky LA deliberately features a guest in advertising.
