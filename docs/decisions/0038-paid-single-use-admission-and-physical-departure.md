# Paid single-use admission and physical departure

October 2, 2026. Isolated implementation; production acceptance remains closed.

The admission desk and existing operations check-in controls now share one authenticated, atomic admission mutation. It verifies stored settlement, canonical positive integer quantities and cents, exact party counts, provider identity and global PaymentIntent ownership, billing exceptions, refund state, the selected Los Angeles date and entry-through-closing entitlement. It then records immutable redemption and counts every age toward30 physically inside. Admin/POS staff can admit; viewer and anonymous callers cannot mutate.

An immutable admission ledger survives cancellation, refund, date rollover and administrative status changes. Entry cannot be undone. Departure records an absolute cumulative number, preserving retries and concurrent partial departures; only actual audited departures reduce physical presence and reservation counts. Full departure never enables re-entry. Unknown historical check-ins/imported booking sources require reconciliation rather than being treated as departures.

Hot occupancy queries read only inside parties and untracked historical check-ins. Active date queries exclude fully departed reservations/bookings and cancelled/released history, with indexed range checks retaining detection of unknown legacy statuses. Physical parties own a conservative whole-day remaining-person span; their booking/reservation spans are suppressed once, including cancellation and extensions to operating hours. Historical completed visits therefore cannot exhaust an admission read limit.

Refund reversal owns only the exact refund-created cancellation, identified by its immutable audit event. Departure changes cannot break this ownership; a later independent staff cancellation clears it. Legacy invalidations without ownership fail closed for reconciliation. Original check-in and departure history remain intact.

The phone ticket displays a QR only when the public paid entitlement remains active; expired, unpaid, cancelled and redeemed tickets have no QR. QR construction retains a four-module quiet zone and offers booking-reference fallback on loading failure. Public links are lookup inputs, never staff authority. Existing Clerk identity/sign-out and route matching are reused. Staff controls clear before uncertain mutations and require another verification; failed refreshes cannot report success. Email labels distinguish provider acceptance, delivery and bounce/complaint/failure.

Evidence is recorded outside the repository in `skydeck-ticketing-research`: actual anonymous local Convex admission races reached exactly30 including27 free children; concurrent duplicate scans produced one redemption; absolute departure retries, role denials, cancellation while inside and no re-entry passed. Payment rows/fixtures were synthetic and staff SDK identities were synthetic, not genuine Stripe payment or Clerk JWT proof. Separate local canonical fixtures rendered paid/used/expired/unpaid phone tickets. Rendered control fixtures use actual presentational components with explicit synthetic disclaimers and no authentication bypass or submitting scripts.
