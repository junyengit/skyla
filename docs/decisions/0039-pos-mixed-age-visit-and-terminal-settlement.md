## Register and capacity

Admission sales use the same canonical $20/$10/free builder as online checkout: adults 18+, youths 8–17 and children 0–7, minimum one adult, maximum 30 people including free children. Staff select an America/Los_Angeles date and offered entry time. The immutable visit, age counts, lines, prices and reader enter the request fingerprint. Café/custom sales remain usable; a café-only sale creates no admission hold or ticket. Ticket lines without a valid visit are refused. All line-count and quantity/money bounds are checked before a hold is acquired.

The public POS mutation authorizes the selected reader and creates the sale plus its all-age hold atomically. Online visits, POS and confirmed full-day private events use the same indexed calendar; actual concurrent last-place requests therefore serialize across channels. Physical entry/departure remains independent of payment and all ages count. Fresh paid fulfillment rereads occupancy even when the owner already has a hold, excluding that hold once. Malformed hours, duplicate/ambiguous owner records, unknown legacy dates or physical evidence, private events and exceeded read bounds remain blockers.

An untouched draft can be explicitly released by its cashier or an admin through `/api/pos/sale-release`, and expires after 30 minutes. Once a Terminal attempt exists, unknown outcomes retain places; there is no cashier override, invented departure or automatic refund.

## Frozen Stripe attempt and settlement

One frozen Terminal attempt records the exact form body, sale/nonce/source, amountUSD, reader/location, Stripe mode, credential fingerprint, provider idempotency key and pinned API `2026-02-25.clover` before its first POST. The public sale and hold enter `payment_pending` in the same transaction. Unknown creation reuses that exact identity within a bounded lease/window; a decline reuses the same known PaymentIntent. Only a narrowly recognized first-request provider rejection proves no intent exists. Timeouts, ambiguous reader responses, false-negative process acknowledgements, capture-required or unsupported states never prove payment failure or permit a fresh charge.

Known intents receive leased provider GET reconciliation, bounded staff refresh and a five-minute repair sweep. Provider credentials/mode must still match the frozen configuration. Automation does not cancel intents. A reader acknowledgement is not payment. Correlated provider cancellation is the unpaid outcome that may release places; processing, action/confirmation-required and payable states keep them.

Signed webhooks and reconciliation share settlement. Modern facts require exact intent, owner, nonce/source, mode, status, captured amount andUSD. Intent/payment identity is globally bounded across all statuses and owners; a foreign pending claim also blocks both ticket bearers. Known money is retained before fulfillment. Duplicate paid identities, malformed line/visit snapshots, mismatched or duplicate holds/bookings/outboxes, late payment and calendar conflicts become durable billing exceptions without issuing admission. Distinct additional paid purchases remain separate money facts. Replays never reset entry/departure or multiply paid rows, tickets or deliveries. Café-only repeats also revalidate their snapshot.

Shared checkout/POS ticket and delivery guards require canonical lines, party, visit, exactly one settled owned payment, exclusive global identity, valid bounded refund evidence and no open money exception. Refund cancellation/restoration additionally requires Terminal payment ID equal to its intent ID, exact owner/provider/currency and safe positive refund totals; ambiguous facts remain visible without changing fulfillment, capacity or attendance. First cancellation and immutable admission history survive later billing events.

## Browser recovery and accessibility

Recovery rereads authoritative state before presenting payment actions. Lost or malformed replies preserve uncertainty. Cart edits/release, repeat reader processing and starting a new sale require the corresponding fresh server state. Foreign cashiers cannot read/refresh/process another cashier’s sale; an admin may inspect it. Viewer/anonymous access stays denied.
